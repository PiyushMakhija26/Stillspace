Stillspace Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Stillspace is a Chrome extension that provides a customizable New Tab page, tab workspaces, local notes, a focus timer, wallpapers, Browser Memory.

Information handled by the extension

Stillspace may access tab titles and URLs to provide features you explicitly use, including saving and restoring tab workspaces and displaying tab information in your workspace manager. The extension may store your preferences, saved workspace details, notes, daily priorities, timer state, and custom wallpapers.

Where data is stored

This information is stored locally in Chrome extension storage on your device. Stillspace's bundled code does not send this information to a Stillspace-operated server. The extension does not include an analytics SDK or advertising SDK.

External services

If you enter a search query in the New Tab search box, Stillspace opens Google Search in a browser tab. Stillspace does not control third-party sites.

Sharing and sale

Stillspace does not sell personal information or transmit locally stored workspace or notes to its developer. Data may be visible to anyone who has access to your browser profile or device.

Your choices and deletion

You choose whether to save tabs, notes, and custom wallpapers. You can delete saved items in the extension interface or remove the extension and its locally stored data through Chrome's extension management settings. Backups you export are saved wherever you choose and are outside the extension's control.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Stillspace's use of information received through Chrome extension APIs is limited to providing the user-facing features described in this policy and the Chrome Web Store listing. Such information is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or transferred to data brokers. The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if Stillspace's data practices change. Before publishing, the developer must add a monitored support contact here and ensure this policy accurately matches the submitted build and Chrome Web Store Privacy practices declarations.
